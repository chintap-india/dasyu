# Academy by chintap

## From ideas to life.

Every great product starts somewhere. A thought, a problem worth solving, a little curiosity, or an idea that refuses to go away.

At Academy by Chintap, we help turn that starting point into something real.

Explore technology, learn by doing, experiment with ideas, build meaningful things, and discover what you're capable of creating.

Imagine · Explore · Learn · Build · Test · Launch

Our Philosophy

## More than learning. It's about making.

Knowing something is one thing. Knowing what to do with it is another.

We believe the best way to learn is to create. To explore possibilities, ask questions, make mistakes, solve problems, and gradually turn ideas into working products.

Develop the habit of asking questions and exploring possibilities.

PILLAR 02

### Creation

PILLAR 04

### Experimentation

PILLAR 05

### Confidence

Gain the practical experience needed to build and ship independently.

Flagship learning programme

## The Full-Stack & Mobile App Development Cohort

Our flagship cohort is an intensive, hands-on learning experience designed to take you from fundamentals to building and deploying modern web and mobile applications.

Duration

12 weeks of structured, project-driven learning.

Format

Small cohorts with direct mentorship and code reviews. Maximum of 15 participants per batch.

Focus

Modern full-stack web, cross-platform mobile, backend APIs, cloud deployment, and AI-assisted workflows.

Outcome

A fully functional, production-ready product designed, built, and shipped by you.

AI as Co-Pilot

Learn how to use modern AI development tools effectively to accelerate your workflow without losing foundational engineering principles.
